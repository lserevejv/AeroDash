How to Play

  • Steer — move the mouse, or use WASD / arrow keys. On touch, drag anywhere.
  • Boost — hold Space, or hold the on-screen boost pad. Orbs refill the meter.
  • Collect — light orbs score points. Glass rings score big and add +1 combo.
  • Ride streams — the glowing ribbons grant speed, bonus score and a multiplier.
  • Checkpoint arches span the whole corridor and can't be missed. Every one pays out; every fourth adds a permanent run multiplier.
  • Portals close a World and open the next. Thread the aperture for a bonus.
  • Shield — clipping a cloud or coral costs one pip and your combo. Clean flying earns pips back. Only a contact with no shield left ends a run.
  • Pause — Esc at any time. Photo mode — P while paused.

There is no timer. A careful pilot can fly forever.
