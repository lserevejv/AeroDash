How to Play

  • Steer — move the mouse, or use WASD / arrow keys. On touch, drag anywhere.
  • Boost — hold Space, or hold the on-screen boost pad. Orbs refill the meter.
  • Collect — light orbs score points. Glass rings score big and add +1 combo.
  • Ride streams — the glowing ribbons grant speed, bonus score and a multiplier.
  • Clouds & coral — brushing one just slows you and resets your combo. Nothing here can end your run.
  • Pause — Esc at any time.

There is no way to lose. Fly for the flow, and for the score.
