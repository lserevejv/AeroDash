Where cyber comes in

I’m Luke. I’m a freshman at UAlbany studying cybersecurity and business management. The part of Aerodash I’d show my cyber class happens after you finish flying.

In one test flight, the results showed 97 Droplets. The wallet went from 2,260 to 2,357 and still showed 2,357 after a refresh. That’s what a player expects. But who controls that number? On this version, the player’s browser does. I’d want a server checking scores before letting people compete.
